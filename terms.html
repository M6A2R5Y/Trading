2. Statistical Forecast Limitations and Model Risk

All price projections, confidence intervals (50% and 90% bands), directional probabilities, Value-at-Risk (VaR), and Expected Shortfall (CVaR) outputs are derived strictly from mathematical formulas applied to historical price series:

  • Non-Stationarity: Financial return distributions exhibit regime shifts, structural breaks, volatility clustering, and fat tails (leptokurtosis) that can invalidate parametric assumptions without warning.
  • Walk-Forward Backtest Scope: Out-of-sample RMSE, MAE, directional hit rates, and Brier scores are calculated over finite historical windows and do not guarantee future predictive performance.
  • Execution Friction: Statistical projections do not account for exchange fees, bid-ask spread widening, orderbook slippage, funding rates, or liquidity outages.

3. Market Data Accuracy and Latency

Live market feeds are sourced from third-party public APIs on an "as available" basis. Network jitter, exchange rate limits, or WebSocket disconnects can delay or interrupt price updates. When live connectivity is unavailable, the terminal switches to an explicitly labeled deterministic offline synthetic series for analytical testing. Never rely on unverified public feeds for automated order execution.
