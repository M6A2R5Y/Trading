Privacy Policy

TickFrame operates as a client-side quantitative market telemetry and statistical modeling workstation. We do not sell personal data, run behavioral advertising trackers, or custody user funds.

1. Architecture and Data Processing Scope

All econometric calculations, including Ordinary Least Squares (OLS) drift estimation, Holt-Winters double exponential smoothing, Autoregressive AR(1) Ornstein-Uhlenbeck modeling, and walk-forward backtests, execute locally inside your browser process.

  • Custom CSV Datasets: When you import an OHLCV CSV file for statistical evaluation, the file contents are parsed strictly in local browser memory (`FileReader` API) and are never transmitted to any external server.
  • Workspace Preferences: Instrument watchlist selections, active bar intervals, and forecast horizon settings are stored locally in your browser using `window.localStorage` under the `tickframe_prefs_v1` key. You can clear this state at any time using your browser settings.

2. Market Data Feed Connections

To display real-time spot market candles and 24-hour orderbook ticker summaries, your browser establishes direct HTTPS and WebSocket connections to public market data endpoints (`data-api.binance.vision` and `data-stream.binance.vision`). Those requests transmit standard network metadata (such as your IP address and User-Agent header) directly to the market data provider in accordance with their public API terms.

3. Cookies and Third-Party Telemetry

TickFrame does not set tracking cookies, cross-site marketing pixels, session recording scripts, or behavioral fingerprinting identifiers. Web fonts (`IBM Plex Sans` and `IBM Plex Mono`) are loaded from Google Fonts over HTTPS.

4. Data Retention and User Rights

Because TickFrame does not maintain a centralized database of user profiles or uploaded financial series, there is no server-side personal record to export or erase. Clearing site data in your browser removes all locally persisted preferences immediately.

5. Contact and Compliance Inquiries

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy or data handling practices may be directed to the systems engineering maintainer via the repository issue tracker or compliance desk at compliance@tickframe.local.
